# NIGHTSHIFT — 30 Online Photo References

Retrieved October 9, 2026. All 30 JPEGs are included in references/photos/. These are online photo references, not images generated for this task. Each entry records its source page and exact downloadable image URL. A few retrieved results linked a collection page; those entries are labeled so the page is not mistaken for an individual photo page.

They do not identify the actual locations. Photographer credits, where needed, should be taken from the linked individual source page; this pack does not invent missing creator names.
